I'm an AI engineer who works at the seam between what a model can do in a notebook and what it has to do in production — under real latency budgets, real data governance, and real users who notice when it's wrong.

Four years in machine learning, the last three focused hard on generative AI. I've owned retrieval pipelines end to end and worked on teams building the full GenAI lifecycle — ingestion, embedding, retrieval, evaluation, and real-time inference — designed to scale across use cases rather than serve one demo.

I don't trust a result until I've tried to break it. That runs through the retrieval systems, the model evaluation, and the numbers I'm willing to put my name on.
